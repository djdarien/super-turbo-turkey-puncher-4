# super-turbo-turkey-puncher-4
Welcome to the ultimate turkey punching experience! Inspired by the beloved mini-game from the classic Doom 3, "Super Turbo Turkey Puncher 4" is a nostalgic yet thrilling sequel to the original. Get ready to unleash your punches on turkeys and rack up the highest scores possible in this fun-filled, action-packed game!


In this rendition, you aim to punch as many Thanksgiving turkeys as you can. With special turkeys that offer more points and a combo system that rewards rapid successive hits, there are endless opportunities to maximize your score. Get immersed in the engaging graphics and background music, and make sure to keep an ear out for the iconic gobble and punch sounds!


##How to Play:


# Starting the Game

Click "Start Game" on the main menu to begin.

The game screen will appear, and turkeys will start spawning.



# Punching Turkeys

Move your mouse to position the fist over a turkey.

Click to punch the turkey and score points.

Each successful punch on a turkey will yield points:

Normal turkey: 100 points







# Game Controls

Pause: Click the "Pause" button at the top right of the screen.

In the pause menu, click "Resume" to continue the game or "Return to Main Menu" to go back.



Exit Game: Click the "Main Menu" button at the top right of the screen.

Confirm your high score when prompted, then return to the main menu.





# High Scores

Check the top scores by clicking the "High Scores" button on the main menu.

Compare your scores against past attempts and see if you can beat your best!



# Controls Summary

Mouse Movement: Move the fist

Mouse Click: Punch

Pause Button: Pause the game

Main Menu Button: Return to the main menu

Enter Key: Quickly exit to the main menu and submit your high score




Whether you're aiming to beat your high score or simply enjoy some turkey-punching action, "Super Turbo Turkey Puncher 4" offers endless fun. Stay tuned for upcoming features like new combos and more ways to score big!
